Enterprise Autonomous Financial Decision Intelligence Platform

An enterprise-grade, multi-agent AI platform for autonomous financial document analysis, compliance validation, risk assessment, portfolio intelligence, and decision support. Built using LangGraph, FastAPI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, and modern observability tools to simulate real-world financial intelligence workflows used in banking and investment firms.

Overview

Financial institutions process thousands of documents, regulatory filings, portfolio reports, and market data sources daily. Manual analysis is time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to scale.

CapitalMind AI automates the financial intelligence workflow through a coordinated team of AI agents capable of:

Analyzing financial reports
Performing compliance checks
Generating risk scores
Retrieving market intelligence
Producing portfolio recommendations
Maintaining explainable and auditable decision trails
